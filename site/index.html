Course Lab

Courses that test the learner. Every chapter ends with a 10-question quiz that grades itself in the page, by level, difficulty and topic. Paste the results back and Claude names the misconceptions, says what to re-read, and builds a retest aimed at the weak spots.

How it works

Learn, test, diagnose, retest

The quiz tells the learner where they stand right away; the evaluation tells them why.

  1. Write the chapter

    Course Studio writes and checks it: run code, an interactive figure, a built page.

  2. Write the quiz

    3 easy, 4 medium, 3 hard from the finished chapter. Every wrong option is a named mistake with a reason, and every answer is computed or run.

  3. Grade in the page

    Weighted score and level, by difficulty and by topic, confident mistakes apart from guesses, and a Revisit link for each weak topic.

  4. Paste back, retest

    Claude reads which wrong options were picked, names the misconception behind them, and builds a retest weighted to the weak topics.

The result

A grade that says what to do next

Easy, medium and hard are worth 1, 2 and 3 points, so the level reflects depth. Mastered needs 85% and at least two of the three hard questions.

Quality bar

Questions you can trust

  • Answerable from the chapter, with exactly one right answer, verified by computing it, running it or tracing the chapter's own code.
  • A real mix: easy is one step, hard is transfer to a case the chapter didn't show, not longer wording.
  • Distractors are mistakes, each with a rationale, so the evaluation can say what the learner believes.
  • Validated: the 3/4/3 mix, answer-position skew, topic spread and every section link are checked before building.
  • Taken headless: all right, all wrong and a mixed attempt on desktop and on phone in dark mode, with grading, links, report and retake checked.
  • Self-contained pages in Course Studio's style, light and dark, with the quiz inside the chapter.
